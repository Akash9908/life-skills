# Listening and Assertive Communication

## What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Focus on the speaker and the topic.
2. Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts or other things around you.
3. Let the speaker finish before responding.
4. Avoid interrupting the other person.
5. Show that you are listening through body language.
6. Paraphrase what the speaker said to check whether you understood the message correctly.

## According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. **Listen with empathy:** Try to understand the situation from the speaker's point of view.
2. **Accept the speaker:** Listen without judging or immediately agreeing or disagreeing.
3. **Be genuine:** Respond honestly instead of pretending to understand or showing fake concern.
4. **Be specific:** Focus on what the speaker is actually saying and feeling.
5. **Reflect the message:** Restate or clarify the speaker's thoughts in your own words.
6. **Use thoughtful silence:** Give the speaker time to think and continue when needed.

## What are the obstacles in your listening process?

1. Sometimes, my mind focuses on what I want to say next instead of listening to the speaker.
2. I may get distracted by my surroundings, phone notifications, or other thoughts.
3. Sometimes, I form an opinion before the speaker has finished explaining.
4. I may interrupt because I think I already understand the point.
5. If the speaker uses unfamiliar words or explains something too quickly, I may miss some details.
6. Sometimes, I focus on the words and miss the speaker's tone or body language.

## What can you do to improve your listening?

1. Keep my phone away and avoid other distractions during conversations.
2. Give the speaker my full attention and avoid thinking about my response while they are speaking.
3. Let the speaker finish before I respond.
4. Ask questions whenever I do not understand something.
5. Pay attention to the speaker's facial expressions, tone, and body language.
6. Take notes during important meetings or technical discussions.
7. Avoid judging the speaker and try to understand their point of view.

## When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

1. When I am afraid that expressing my opinion may lead to an argument.
2. When I agree with others even though I have a different opinion.
3. When I find it difficult to say no to someone, even when I am busy.
4. When I avoid asking questions because I am worried about what others might think.
5. When I stay quiet even though I have something important to say.

## When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

1. When I become frustrated because someone does not understand my point.
2. When I feel that someone is repeatedly ignoring what I say.
3. When I am under pressure and respond without thinking calmly.
4. When I interrupt someone because I strongly believe that my opinion is correct.
5. When I blame others instead of explaining the problem calmly.

## When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

1. When I feel hurt or angry but do not express it directly.
2. When I use sarcasm instead of explaining what is bothering me.
3. When I give silent treatment because I do not want to discuss an issue.
4. When I make indirect comments instead of clearly saying what I feel.
5. When I complain about someone to others instead of discussing the problem with that person.

## How can you make your communication assertive?

1. **Recognize and name my feelings:** Understand what I am actually feeling and express it clearly instead of blaming others.
2. **Understand my needs:** Identify the reason behind what I am asking for, rather than focusing only on the immediate request.
3. **Start with simple situations:** Practise speaking up with people I feel comfortable with before handling more difficult conversations.
4. **Pay attention to body language and tone:** Use a calm voice, suitable body language, and clear words so that I do not sound either passive or aggressive.
5. **Speak up early:** Address problems and express my needs before they become repeated issues.
6. **Respect other people's feelings:** Express my opinions clearly while also listening to and respecting the other person's point of view.
