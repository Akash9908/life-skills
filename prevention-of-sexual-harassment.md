# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Some examples include:

1. Unwanted physical contact, touching, or inappropriate advances.
2. Asking for sexual favours or making unwanted sexual requests.
3. Making inappropriate comments, jokes, or remarks about someone's body or appearance.
4. Showing or sharing sexually explicit images, videos, or messages.
5. Making inappropriate gestures or staring at someone in a way that makes them uncomfortable.
6. Sending unwanted sexual messages, emails, or comments.
7. Threatening someone or promising benefits based on their willingness to accept sexual advances.
8. Repeatedly making someone uncomfortable through unwanted behaviour, even after they have expressed that they are not comfortable with it.

## Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I ever face such behaviour, I would first make it clear to the person that their behaviour is inappropriate and that I am not comfortable with it, provided it is safe to do so. I would also keep a record of the incidents, including the dates, times, locations, what happened, and the names of any witnesses. If the behaviour continues, I would report it in writing to a member of the company's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). If I witness someone else facing harassment, I would support them and encourage them to report the incident rather than ignore it. I believe it is important to speak up against such behaviour and help maintain a safe and respectful workplace for everyone.
