# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit is the ability to stay committed to a long-term goal and keep working towards it, even when things become difficult. It is not the talent or the IQ that makes you successful, but it is the grit that makes you successful. One should think about their future, like it is a marathon and not a sprint, and it is what helps them grow. One should take the lessons from their failures and get back up, as failure is not permanent.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that our skills and abilities are not fixed and can be improved through effort, learning, and practice. Instead of avoiding challenges or worrying about failure, we should treat them as opportunities to learn and improve.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, efforts, and decisions have an influence on the outcomes in our lives. The key point is that we should focus on the things we can control instead of blaming external factors. When we solve problems through our own efforts and recognize the improvements we make, we develop a stronger belief in ourselves, which helps us stay motivated.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own curriculum for growth.
4. Honor the struggle.

## 5. Mindset - A MountBlue Warrior Reference Manual

I want to build a growth mindset by focusing more on learning and improving rather than worrying about how quickly I can achieve something. I will try to understand concepts deeply instead of just memorizing solutions. For example, when I struggle to solve a DSA problem or understand a new concept, I will try different approaches, refer to documentation, and learn from my mistakes instead of giving up. I will also take feedback positively, ask questions whenever I have doubts, and regularly practise the topics I learn. Most importantly, I want to be consistent with my efforts and understand that making mistakes or facing difficulties is a normal part of learning.
