The feynman technique is nothing but, you should be able explain a topic to someone who doesn't have the same base knowledge on the topic as you and they should be able to understand it.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active Mode of Thinking:** It is a mode of thinking where we concentrate on a particular problem or topic. We use this mode when we are actively learning, solving problems, or trying to understand something.

- **Diffused Mode of Thinking:** It is a mode of thinking where the brain makes connections between different ideas without concentrating on a specific problem. It can happen when we take a walk, relax, or engage in activities that do not require intense concentration.

2. I will try to explain every concept in my own words, as if I am teaching someone who has no prior knowledge of it.

4. I will avoid distractions such as mobile notifications and social media while studying.

6. I will work on improving my communication skills by explaining technical concepts clearly and discussing the difficulties I face.
