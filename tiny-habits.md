## 2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP stands for **Behavior = Motivation, Ability and Prompt**. According to this model, a habit happens when these three things come together.

- **M - Motivation:** The reason or desire to do something.
- **A - Ability:** How easy it is for us to do that particular task.
- **P - Prompt:** A reminder that tells us when to do it.

We can use this model by starting with a small habit and connecting it to something we already do every day.

### Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or celebrate after completing a habit?

I think celebrating small achievements is important because it makes us feel good about what we have done. When we feel happy after completing a task, we are more likely to do it again. We don't need to celebrate in a big way. Even a small appreciation like saying "Good job!" to ourselves is enough. It helps us notice our progress instead of feeling that our efforts are not making any difference.

## 4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits

### Question 5: What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book explains that building good habits is not just about achieving a goal. It is also about changing how we see ourselves. Instead of saying that I want to solve more coding problems, I should try to become someone who practises problem-solving regularly. Every time I complete a small task related to that goal, I am building that identity. I understood that our daily actions play an important role in becoming the person we want to be.

### Question 6: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.

The book explains that we should make good habits as easy as possible to follow. If a task requires too much effort, we may keep postponing it. So, we should prepare things in advance and start with smaller tasks.

### Question 7: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.

The book suggests making bad habits difficult to follow by adding extra steps or removing things that trigger them. This way, we won't do them automatically.Like, logging out of social media applications or set app limits. This adds an extra step before opening them and may help me avoid unnecessary scrolling.

## 5. Reflection

### Question 8: Pick one habit that you would like to do more of. What steps can you take to make the cue obvious, the habit attractive and easy, and the response satisfying?

One habit I want to develop is practising DSA problems every day. Sometimes, I plan to do it, but every time, I see something hard or something I don't know, I will go back to something, that is easy for me, like scrolling. To improve this, I can keep my laptop ready on my desk and decide to solve at least one problem after breakfast. This will act as a reminder.

I can make it more interesting by tracking the problems I solve and noticing my progress. Instead of setting a target that is difficult to achieve, I will start with one problem a day. After completing it, I can mark it as done in a tracker. This will give me a small sense of achievement and encourage me to continue the habit.

### Question 9: Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of. What steps can you take to make the cue invisible, the process unattractive or difficult, and the response unsatisfying?

One habit I want to reduce is spending too much time scrolling through social media, especially during study hours. Many times, I open an application just to check something and end up spending much more time than I planned.

To reduce this, I can turn off unnecessary notifications and keep my phone away from my study table. I can also log out of social media applications or set daily usage limits. Whenever I feel bored while studying, instead of immediately picking up my phone, I can take a short break or go for a walk. I can also check my screen time at the end of the day to understand how much time I am spending on these applications. These small changes can help me control this habit.
